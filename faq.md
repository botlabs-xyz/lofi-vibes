## FAQ

### Is Lofi Vibes playlist-focused?

Lofi Vibes is positioned as ambient radio-style listening, not deep queue management.

### Is it good for study servers?

Yes. Study and co-working rooms are a primary use case.

### Where do I verify live command names?

Use `/help` in your server for the currently deployed command set.

### Where can I get help?

Use the official [Support Server](https://discord.gg/BusuZp2G8w).
