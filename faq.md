## FAQ

### Is Lofi Vibes free to use?

Yes. Normal radio, music, profile, station switching, and 24/7 features are free-first. Optional supporter membership is community recognition only.

### Is Lofi Vibes playlist-focused?

No. Lofi Vibes is primarily a live radio and station bot. It is built for continuous background listening rather than deep playlist management.

### How do I change stations?

Use `/station`, then choose a preset station from the dropdown. The bot should switch to the newly selected station without needing to disconnect first.

### How do I keep music running?

Use `/24-7 mode:enable`. It saves the server preference and attempts to recover the active or saved station when possible.

### Can users suggest stations?

Yes. Use `/submit-station`. Suggestions go to a private bot-team review channel before anything is submitted externally.

### Where do I verify live command names?

Use `/help` in your server for the command set currently registered by your deployment.
